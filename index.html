BEFORE YOU PROCEED

Facility Notices

How does a purchase work?

Each "Request Clearance" button takes you to the official Roblox Game Pass page for that level. All payment is handled by Roblox using Robux — nothing is processed on this site.

Do I need Robux?

Yes. Clearance levels are purchased entirely with Robux through Roblox's own platform, the same as any other Game Pass.

Refunds

Refunds follow Roblox's standard Game Pass policies, since all transactions take place on Roblox — not through MRF directly.

Is this official?

This is the official storefront for Medwood Research Facility on Roblox. MRF is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation.

Age requirement

You need a Roblox account with an account age of 16 or older to buy a clearance level. Roblox enforces this, not MRF.
